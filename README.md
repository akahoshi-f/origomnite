## About

Origomnite is a 3D action RPG developed as a Final Year Project for the Digital Game Programming technical course at ETEC Parque da Juventude, São Paulo, Brazil. The project received the highest grade (MB - Very Good) from the evaluation committee.

The game features a morally gray protagonist in a fantasy world inspired by Brazilian culture, geography, and folklore. Through an innovative narrative approach, the game challenges players to reflect on their actions, their consequences for others, and the ethical definitions of good and evil.

### Project Objectives

- Present Brazilian cultural representation through landscapes, folklore, architecture, and biodiversity in a fantasy setting
- Develop a narrative that encourages moral reflection on the motivations behind player actions
- Promote diversity and inclusion through a female protagonist with vitiligo
- Apply technical knowledge acquired throughout the Digital Game Programming course

### Development Context

Developed over six months by a team of eight students, the project demonstrates the application of both technical and interpersonal skills acquired during three years of study. The team successfully balanced high school academic requirements with technical course demands while delivering a functional game demo.

## Narrative

The game follows Lana, an 18-year-old adventurer who steals a magical stone believing she is protecting the world. As players progress through diverse Brazilian-inspired landscapes, they encounter various characters and face moral challenges.

The narrative employs a unique perspective: events are presented through Lana's understanding, portraying her as a hero throughout the journey. Only at the conclusion must players confront the reality of their actions and their devastating consequences for the world.

### Core Themes

- Moral ambiguity and the complexity of ethical decision-making
- Environmental impact and human responsibility toward nature
- Personal growth through self-awareness and accountability
- Cultural identity and Brazilian heritage representation

## Technologies

### Development Tools

| Technology | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **Godot Engine 4** | Primary game engine for development and programming |
| **Blender** | 3D modeling, rigging, and animation |
| **Krita** | 2D artwork, concept art, and texture creation |
| **CapCut** | Video editing for cutscenes |
| **Canva** | Marketing materials and promotional content |
| **FL Studio** | Original soundtrack composition |

### Technical Implementation

**Programming Language**: GDScript (Godot's native language with Python-like syntax)

**Architecture**: Node-based system allowing modular development where each game element (characters, environments, UI) can be developed independently and integrated into the main scene hierarchy.

**Art Style**: Low-poly 3D aesthetic with reduced polygon count for optimized performance. Color palettes designed to convey emotional tone and narrative context.

**Audio**: Original soundtrack composed by Ayano (Igor Mateus da Silva) using FL Studio. Sound effects sourced from royalty-free libraries.

### Controls

**Keyboard & Mouse**
- WASD: Movement
- Shift: Sprint
- Space: Jump
- Left Mouse Button: Attack (3-hit combo)
- Right Mouse Button: Special Attack (consumes stamina)
- E: Interact with NPCs
- ESC: Pause menu

**Gamepad**
- Left Analog Stick: Movement
- R1: Sprint
- X (PlayStation) / A (Xbox): Jump
- Square (PlayStation) / X (Xbox): Attack
- Circle (PlayStation) / B (Xbox): Special Attack
- Triangle (PlayStation) / Y (Xbox): Interact
- Start: Pause menu

## Design Influences

The development team drew inspiration from established indie titles:

- **Undertale**: Moral choice systems and memorable soundtrack composition
- **Celeste**: Chapter presentation aesthetics and emotional narrative design
- **Spiritfarer**: 2D scenic design and atmospheric storytelling
- **Stardew Valley**: Character development and interconnected narrative threads
- **Genshin Impact**: Environmental design, combat mechanics, and immersive audio
